# Price Reference Decision Record

**Status:** Approved for provisional use

**Decision date:** 4 September 2026

**Market represented:** United States
**Purpose:** Explain what voluntary software support can buy for the maker. These values are comparisons, not local restaurant quotations and not the amount charged unless the checkout explicitly shows that amount.

## Approved baseline

| Comparison | Reference amount |
|---|---:|
| One estimated fry | About $0.05 |
| Vanilla cone | $2.31 |
| Hamburger | $2.89 |
| Hamburger Happy Meal | $6.19 |
| Quarter Pounder with Cheese meal | $12.19 |

The fry estimate uses a $3.56 published U.S. mean for medium fries and an independent small experiment reporting an average of 112 grams and 78 fries across 10 orders from five franchises in one city.

Unrounded calculation:

`$3.56 / 78 = $0.045641 per fry`

Equivalent mass calculation:

`112 g / 78 = 1.435897 g per fry`

`($3.56 / 112 g) x 1.435897 g = $0.045641 per fry`

The user-facing comparison rounds this to **about $0.05**. It must remain labeled as an estimate until replaced by a retained measurement study.

## Required public qualification

> Independent U.S. menu-price references retrieved September 4, 2026. Prices vary by restaurant and ordering channel and may exclude tax and fees. These comparisons are not a quote for your nearest restaurant. The exact contribution is shown before payment.

Do not describe these figures as official nationwide McDonald's prices, current prices at the user's nearest restaurant, or a promise that the item can be purchased locally for that amount.

## Privacy boundary

Do not retain restaurant addresses, store numbers, GPS coordinates, precise user location, nearby-store results, IP-derived location, or personal information for this comparison. If regional pricing is introduced, use only a broad area and do not require precise location merely to show the support page.

## Correction procedure

If a material source or calculation error is found:

1. Disable or replace the affected comparison promptly.
2. Identify the previous statement, corrected statement, affected dates or releases, and reason.
3. Publish a plain correction wherever the inaccurate claim materially influenced a payment request.
4. Preserve the minimum prior record required to explain the correction.
5. Do not silently rewrite historical evidence.

This record approves the reference set for provisional implementation. It is not a legal opinion, an affiliation claim, or permission to obscure the actual checkout charge.
